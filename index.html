Words to Know

Stipend

A regular fixed allowance paid to a student to cover basic living expenses (housing, food, transport) during their studies.

Tuition Waiver

An agreement where the university or scholarship body forgives 100% or a percentage of your academic tuition fees.

Apostille / Legalization

Official embassy or government authentication verifying that your degree certificate and transcript are genuine for international use.

Letter of Expected Graduation

An official document from your university registrar confirming you are in your final year and scheduled to graduate before master's enrollment.

Application Timeline (12 Months Before Enrollment)

Sept – Oct

Program Selection & AI Fact-Checking

Select 5–8 target programs. Check eligibility, language requirements, and official scholarship deadlines.

Nov – Dec

Testing & Document Drafts

Take TOEFL/IELTS if required. Draft your Statement of Purpose (SOP) and request recommendation letters from IBSC professors.

Jan – Mar

Official Portal Submission

Submit online applications and government scholarship applications (e.g., MOE Taiwan, MEXT Japan, GKS Korea, Erasmus).

Apr – Jun

Document Legalization & Visa

Receive admission letters. Request diploma legalization from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) and apply for student visas.
